# Goal G32 — Wave 32: document-level takes

**Status:** complete (2026-11-24).
**Tracker:** `TODO.md` §1 (takes and per-take fps).
**Depends on:** none.
**Roadmap:** [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## Definition of done

- Takes live on `Document`, not per node, with an active take and per-take fps.
- Old `.lcscene` files migrate into a default take and keep evaluating.
- The Takes tab edits document-level takes, and glTF export still reads the
  active take.
- All lane checks and the full gate pass.

## Outcome

- `Document` owns `takes: Vec<Take>` and `active_take`, with each `Take`
  carrying a name, fps and `TakeClips` for node, per-tag and material clips.
  Live `Node::animation`, `Node::tag_animations` and `Material::animation`
  remain the working view; activating a take stores the live clips into the
  old take and restores the new one.
- Files without a take list migrate into one default `Take 1` at load without
  changing evaluation. Save, load and `Document::signature` normalize the
  active take, and finite take fps clamps to `1..=240` while non-finite values
  are refused.
- `SetTakesCmd` wraps create, rename, duplicate, delete, activate and fps as one
  undo step each. Deleting the active take activates a neighbour; deleting the
  last take creates a fresh default.
- The Takes tab lists document takes, has New/Duplicate/Delete, Rename and
  Apply FPS controls, and clicking a row activates that take. Every action
  recalculates `LeopardCraftApp` playback from the active take's fps. Invalid
  names and non-finite fps leave history untouched.
- glTF export uses the active take's name and fps, writes them in animation and
  root `extras`, and import reads that metadata; files without it keep the
  default take. The GLB test asserts the take name, fps, extras and sampled
  channel counts.
- Tests: document take migration/switching/fps/undo/round-trip signature in
  `lc-scene`, editor take operations and playback/refusal in `lc-ui`, and the
  GLB take-name/fps assertions in `lc-io`.
