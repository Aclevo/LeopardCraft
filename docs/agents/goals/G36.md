# Goal G36 — Wave 36: modifier and subdivision parity

**Status:** complete (2026-11-24). The Cage/Type controls, crease weights,
partial subdivision, crease visibility outside Edge mode, the six missing
modifiers (Displacement, Formula, Magnet, Laplacian Smooth, Polygon Reduction,
Wrap) and the exact Stam-Loop hybrid are implemented and gated. Stam and Loop's
scheme is one unified linear-split-plus-averaging operator: each iteration
splits every n-gon into n quads around its centroid, applies Catmull-Clark masks
on quad-only rings and Loop masks on triangle-only rings, and applies the
paper's mixed mask where the two meet, so a mixed cage is never triangulated.
The optional Table 1 vertex correction is deliberately omitted because each pure
region already uses its exact mask, and a quad/triangle edge takes the mean of
the two edge rules; the research is recorded in `TODO.md` §6.
